I'm a Machine Learning Engineer

I Build Data-Driven Solutions for Real-World Impact

In 2021, I read Bernard Marr's Forbes article, “How Is AI Used In Healthcare - 5 Powerful Real-World Examples That Show The Latest Advances,” which showcased groundbreaking AI applications like robotic surgery and virtual nursing assistants. This exposure ignited my passion for AI and led me to pursue a Master of Science in Machine Learning and Data Science at UC San Diego immediately after my undergraduate studies.

I am deeply passionate about leveraging data and machine learning to solve complex problems and drive innovation. I am excited about exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science, and cloud computing. Through my internships at the San Diego Supercomputer Center and Hewlett Packard Enterprise, I’ve gained hands-on experience with machine learning, deep learning, and virtualized environments. I’m always eager to learn new techniques, collaborate on impactful projects, and push the boundaries of what's possible in the world of AI and data science.
